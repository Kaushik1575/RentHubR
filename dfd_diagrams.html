Overall boundary interaction between External Entities and RentHub Core
Level 0 • Context
Explanation for Word Report: The Context Diagram illustrates the boundary of the RentHub system. The entire system is abstracted into a single central process (0.0 RentHub System). It interacts with two primary external terminators: the Registered Customer (providing login credentials, search filters, and booking transactions) and the System Administrator (managing fleet inventory, promotional rewards, and system configurations).

Figure 2.2: DFD Level 1 — Functional Decomposition Diagram

Decomposition into 5 Core Functional Sub-processes and 6 Central Data Stores
Level 1 • Decomposition
Explanation for Word Report: DFD Level 1 decomposes the central system into five fundamental operational processes: 1.0 User Authentication, 2.0 Fleet Management, 3.0 Booking & Reservation, 4.0 Offers & Rewards, and 5.0 Support & Grievance Handling. Data flows directly between processes and dedicated relational stores (D1: `users`, D2: `admins`, D3: Fleet Catalog, D4: `bookings`, D5: `offers`/`rewards`, D6: `issues`).

Granular Step-by-Step Transaction Pipeline for Vehicle Booking & Billing
Level 2 • Subsystem
